HONEYPOT

The CEO Survival Simulator

AI Summit, Singapore. You run the world’s most valuable chip company and the prototype sits in a glass case in the middle of the ballroom. Some of the people smiling at you were sent by Chinese state security, a rival chipmaker or a data broker. Find them before they find what they came for.

Protect your chips. Resist temptation. Don’t take the bait.

Follow @PlayHoneyPotlol

Who’s in the room

Hundreds of people pass through the ballroom over the year. Anyone in any role can be an operative, and most of them aren’t.

Executives and investorsPartnership talk, funding offers and the occasional roadmap question that goes one step too far.
Models and gala guestsThe most glamorous people at the summit, and the ones who most want a private dinner.
Journalists and photographersThey want quotes and close-ups. Some want your badge in the frame.
Researchers and your own engineersThe insider threat looks exactly like your team.
Caterers and staffNobody looks twice at the person holding the tray.
Security, keynote and barTwo guards answer your phone. The keynote speaker and bartender stay put.
